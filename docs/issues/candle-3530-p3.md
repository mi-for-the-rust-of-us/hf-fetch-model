@ivarflakstad, since those numbers came from my earlier [reply](https://github.com/huggingface/candle/issues/3530#issuecomment-4441304624), three notes that may help decide, without answering for @sempervictus:

**1. The model-size figure still holds, with the same caveat.** Re-running `hf-fm inspect nvidia/Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B-Instruct-NVFP4 --dtypes` today gives the same 47.24 GiB across 11 shards (U8 36.26 GiB, BF16 6.45 GiB, F8_E4M3 4.53 GiB, F32 577.6 KiB). It is still a guess at the model from the crash log; the reporter hasn't named it.

**2. The ~14 GiB "runtime / RSS overhead" is a remainder, not a measurement.** It is what is left after subtracting the weights, the KV pool and the Mamba state from `used`: 81 − 47.24 − 18.00 − 2.06 = 13.70 GiB with the rounded `free -g` figure, or 14.79 GiB with the crash-time `free -m` (84,061 MiB). It lumps together everything not itemized (CUDA context, workspaces, allocator slack, any page cache counted as used), so I wouldn't read an inefficiency into it without measuring the process directly.

**3. The KV-pool size, and the refusal, come from the engine on top of candle.** In the current source of [xinfer](https://github.com/guoqingbao/xinfer) (formerly vllm.rs, which the backtrace names), the "please request later" error is a `candle_core::bail!` in its own [`LLMEngine`](https://github.com/guoqingbao/xinfer/blob/2c37e11aa106687a5d7dffeee5c22329a96cad1a/src/core/engine.rs#L767-L775), which is why `candle_core::error::Error::bt` appears in the trace: candle supplies the error type. The pool itself is sized in its [`kvcache_allocator`](https://github.com/guoqingbao/xinfer/blob/2c37e11aa106687a5d7dffeee5c22329a96cad1a/src/utils/kvcache_allocator.rs#L1128-L1160) as the free memory `cuMemGetInfo_v2` reports after loading, times `kv_fraction` ([0.6 by default under CUDA, 0.7 with flash attention](https://github.com/guoqingbao/xinfer/blob/2c37e11aa106687a5d7dffeee5c22329a96cad1a/src/utils/kvcache_allocator.rs#L336-L346), [0.8 when `max_model_len` is set](https://github.com/guoqingbao/xinfer/blob/2c37e11aa106687a5d7dffeee5c22329a96cad1a/src/utils/kvcache_allocator.rs#L574-L581)), less a workspace reserve; the rest is [deliberately left as runtime headroom](https://github.com/guoqingbao/xinfer/blob/2c37e11aa106687a5d7dffeee5c22329a96cad1a/src/utils/kvcache_allocator.rs#L881-L897). So part of the free memory the reporter saw is unused by design, and what remains open (how large the pool should be on a Spark's unified memory, and what `cuMemGetInfo` reports there) is a question about that allocator rather than about how candle maps the model. The source has changed since May (the error's wording differs from the log), so the details may not match the build that crashed.

On that reading, closing here and taking the pool-sizing question to xinfer seems reasonable to me, unless @sempervictus has seen the gap with candle alone.
